Our Story

At Hotel Amudhabawan (HAB) in Sampath Nagar, Erode, we uphold the sacred tradition of 100% pure vegetarian hospitality. In South India, "Hotel" proudly stands for hearty culinary warmth, and our establishment serves exclusively vegetarian food crafted with the utmost reverence for taste and health.

Whether it is our golden, aroma-filled Ghee Podi Roast, steaming hot soft idlies, royal banana leaf meals, or our Indo-Chinese starters like Gobi 65 and Paneer Manchurian, every plate celebrates fresh farm produce and authentic kitchen recipes.

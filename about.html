The Story of Hotel Amudhabawan

Serving the authentic flavors of Kongu and South India since 2008 with unwavering dedication to traditional 100% pure vegetarian culinary art.

🍌 Unlimited Banana Leaf Meals

A Heritage of Purity and Flavor

In South India, the term "Hotel" represents a cherished tradition of dining and hospitality. Located opposite Kongu Kalaiarangam in Sampath Nagar, Erode, Hotel Amudhabawan (HAB) stands as a landmark for 100% pure vegetarian cuisine.

We strictly serve exclusively vegetarian food with zero non-veg options. From dawn to dusk, our kitchens come alive with the sound of sputtering mustard seeds, the fragrant crackle of curry leaves, and the golden sizzle of our legendary Ghee Podi Roast on traditional iron tawa.

Whether you visit for an energizing breakfast of steaming mallipoo idlies, an afternoon feast of unlimited South Indian meals on fresh banana leaves, or an evening gathering with North Indian gravies and Indo-Chinese starters, every preparation is crafted using pure cow ghee, fresh cold-pressed oils, and farm-fresh ingredients.

Our Sacred Pillars

We believe that good food nourishes both body and soul. Our commitment to cleanliness, pure cow ghee, and uncompromised taste has made Hotel Amudhabawan the go-to dining choice for families across Erode and travelers throughout Tamil Nadu.
